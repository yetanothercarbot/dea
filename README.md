A brief attempt at analysing the extents of Lismore's 2017 floods with Sentinel-1. Two approaches are contained within, one based on Digital Earth Australia (2024)'s coastal mapping approach and the other based on Krishna Vanama & Rao (2019)'s Normalised Change Index methodology. Completed as part of a remote sensing course.


**References**
